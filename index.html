About

From scientific questions to reproducible computational tools.

This portfolio brings together software, analysis pipelines, computational frameworks, and educational resources developed across bioinformatics and computational science. The emphasis is on transparent methodology, reusable code, and research workflows that can be inspected, reproduced, and extended.

Projects range from genomic and protein sequence analysis to transcriptomic state-transition modeling, chaos and instability prediction, scientific machine learning, and companion resources for bioinformatics textbooks.
